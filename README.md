## 👨‍💻 Professional Summary

I am a **Full-Stack Engineer** and **Systems Architect** currently pursuing a B.E. in Computer Engineering at **BIET, Davanagere**. 

I specialize in the end-to-end development of **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, Point-of-Sale (POS) platforms, and modern web applications**. My engineering focus is heavily centered around clean architecture, responsive client-side design, modular RESTful APIs, and highly scalable database infrastructure.

> *"Engineering solutions that are not just functional, but scalable, secure, and architecturally sound."*
